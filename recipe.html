Garlic Butter Shrimp Toast

Crunchy toast and juicy shrimp with almost no cleanup.

  • 15 min
  • Serves 2
  • Contains shellfish
  • Beginner

Ingredients

  • 250 g large shrimp, peeled
  • 4 slices crusty bread
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Toast the bread until golden and set it aside.
  2. Melt the butter over medium heat and cook the garlic for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the shrimp and cook 2 to 3 minutes per side until pink.
  4. Stir in the lemon juice and parsley, then season with salt and pepper.
  5. Spoon the shrimp and butter over the toast and serve right away.

Veggie Fried Rice

Day-old rice and whatever vegetables you have.

  • 30 min
  • Serves 3
  • Vegetarian
  • Beginner

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked day-old rice
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 3 green onions, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

Steps

  1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pan and scramble the eggs. Remove and set aside.
  2. Add the remaining oil, then cook the garlic and peas and carrots for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the rice and press it into the pan. Stir and cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the soy sauce, sesame oil, eggs and green onions. Toss and serve.

Slow-Roasted Tomato Soup

Deep, sweet flavor from a patient roast.

  • 55 min
  • Serves 4
  • Vegetarian
  • Intermediate

Ingredients

  • 1 kg ripe tomatoes, halved
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves, unpeeled
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 500 ml vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil to serve

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200 C (400 F). Spread the tomatoes, onion and garlic on a tray, drizzle with oil and season.
  2. Roast for 40 minutes until soft and lightly browned.
  3. Squeeze the garlic from its skins and move everything to a pot with the stock and sugar.
  4. Simmer for 10 minutes, then blend until smooth.
  5. Taste, adjust the seasoning and serve with basil.

Chickpea Curry

Warm spices, creamy sauce, one pan.

  • 40 min
  • Serves 4
  • Vegan
  • Intermediate

Ingredients

  • 2 cans chickpeas, drained
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 400 g canned chopped tomatoes
  • 200 ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh cilantro to serve

Steps

  1. Heat the oil and cook the onion for 8 minutes until soft.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger and curry powder and stir for 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Add the chickpeas and coconut milk and simmer for 15 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and top with cilantro. Serve with rice.

Stovetop Mac & Cheese

Creamy, no baking and no boxed mix.

  • 45 min
  • Serves 4
  • Vegetarian
  • Beginner

Ingredients

  • 250 g elbow macaroni
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 500 ml milk
  • 200 g grated cheddar
  • 1/2 tsp mustard powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cook the macaroni in salted water until just tender, then drain.
  2. Melt the butter in the pot, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Slowly whisk in the milk and simmer for 5 minutes until thick.
  4. Off the heat, stir in the cheddar, mustard powder, salt and pepper.
  5. Return the macaroni to the pot, stir well and serve.

Lemon Garlic Spaghetti

Bright, silky and made in one pot.

  • 20 min
  • Serves 2
  • Vegetarian
  • Beginner

Ingredients

  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 30 g grated parmesan
  • Salt, pepper and chili flakes to taste

Steps

  1. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until just al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water.
  2. Warm the olive oil over low heat and cook the garlic for 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant, not brown.
  3. Add the drained pasta, lemon zest, juice and a splash of pasta water. Toss for 1 minute.
  4. Off the heat, stir in the parmesan, pepper and chili flakes. Loosen with pasta water if needed.
